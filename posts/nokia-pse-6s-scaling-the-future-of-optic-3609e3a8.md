# Nokia PSE-6s: Scaling the Future of Optical Networking

> Nokia’s sixth‑generation super‑coherent Photonic Service Engine (PSE‑6s) leverages 5 nm silicon and CSTAR silicon photonics to deliver up to 1.2 Tb/s per wavelength, 50 % higher capacity, and the ability to bond two wavelengths for 2.4 Tb/s transport, enabling single‑line‑card delivery of three 800 GE services and up to 25 % more fiber capacity. It extends 800 GE reach to 2 000 km—tripling current distances—while cutting interface count and eliminating many regenerators, and it reduces power per bit by 40 % and overall network power consumption by up to 60 % through greater efficiency and longer reach. Integrated telemetry and AI‑driven analytics provide real‑time monitoring, fault detection, and automated recovery, creating a smarter, more sustainable optical network for the growing 800 GE era.

# **Nokia PSE-6s: Opening New Frontiers in Scale, Performance, and Sustainability**

In an era where technology evolves through successive waves of innovation, optical networking has reached a critical juncture. For years, the industry has relied on coherent optical transmission to overcome the plateaus of the past, but today, we face the **Shannon Limit**—a theoretical maximum of spectral efficiency. As network operators grapple with exponential bandwidth growth, the need for a new approach that balances massive scale with environmental sustainability has never been more urgent. Nokia’s response to this challenge is the **sixth-generation super-coherent Photonic Service Engine (PSE-6s)**, a breakthrough designed to redefine the economics of global networks.

## **The Technological Foundation: 5nm Silicon and CSTAR**

At the heart of the PSE-6s is the industry’s most advanced optical technology. By harnessing **5-nanometer silicon integrated circuit technology**, Nokia has developed a coherent Digital Signal Processor (DSP) that pushes the boundaries of what is possible in a digital coherent optic (DCO) module. This DSP is closely integrated with **CSTAR silicon photonics**, enabling the PSE-6s to support the fastest wavelength speeds available today at **1.2Tb/s**.

This isn't just about raw speed; it's about intelligence and efficiency. The PSE-6s utilizes third-generation **Probabilistic Constellation Shaping (PCS)**, a sophisticated algorithm that optimizes how data is encoded onto light waves. When combined with a **continuously adjustable baud rate** and variable-gain forward error correction (FEC), the PSE-6s can be fine-tuned to achieve the highest capacity and transmission performance in real-world network conditions.

### **Pillar 1: Unprecedented Scale for the 800GE Era**

The demand for bandwidth is projected to grow by over 40% annually through 2027, driven by 5G, video consumption, and emerging applications like AR/VR and autonomous vehicles. This surge requires a migration to high-speed **400GE and 800GE** IP router interfaces.

The PSE-6s provides a massive leap in scale, increasing wavelength capacity by **50% over existing solutions**. One of its most innovative features is the ability to enable chip-to-chip communication between two PSE-6s modules, allowing operators to **bond two wavelengths into a single 2.4Tb/s optical transport solution** on one line card.

For metro networks, this means a single line card can transport **3 x 800GE services**—three times the capacity of current solutions. This efficiency allows operators to scale their networks while simultaneously reducing equipment footprint, deployment costs, and overall power consumption. In total, the PSE-6s can deliver up to **25% more total fiber capacity**.

### **Pillar 2: Redefining Performance and Network Economics**

Scale is only half of the equation; that capacity must also be transportable over meaningful distances. The PSE-6s sets new benchmarks in optical performance by operating at **130 Gbaud or more**.

The most game-changing performance metric is the ability to transport **800GE services over a single wavelength across distances of up to 2,000 km**. This represents **three times the reach** of current coherent solutions. For service providers with national, continental, or subsea networks, this achievement has profound economic implications:

- **Reduced Interface Count:** It reduces the number of coherent optics needed for long-haul transport by up to **50%**.

- **Elimination of Regenerators:** By extending the reach at 800G, the PSE-6s practically eliminates the need for costly intermediate regenerators in many regional and long-haul paths.

- **Simplified Architecture:** It avoids the complexity of splitting 800GE services across multiple wavelengths, making the network easier to manage.

### **Pillar 3: A New Standard for Sustainability**

As climate change puts the planet under stress, network operators are increasingly focused on sustainability and reducing "power per bit". The PSE-6s is designed to be **"faster, farther, and greener"**.

By leveraging the latest 5nm silicon and Moore’s Law, the PSE-6s consumes **40% less power per bit** than existing solutions. However, the sustainability benefits are multiplicative. Because the PSE-6s can increase transmission reach threefold, operators need fewer optics to cover the same distance. When these factors are combined, the PSE-6s can reduce total network power consumption by up to **60% in regional and long-haul networks**. This allows operators to meet the dual goals of increasing capacity and meeting strict carbon footprint reduction targets.

## **Enabling a Smarter, Automated Network**

Beyond its physical performance, the PSE-6s acts as a sophisticated sensor for the entire fiber infrastructure. It utilizes **advanced streaming telemetry** to monitor the light as it traverses fiber links, providing unprecedented insight into the behavior of the network and its environment.

Crucially, this monitoring happens **without the need for additional specialized instruments**, lowering the cost of network oversight. This telemetry data is fed into the **Nokia WaveSuite Health and Analytics** solution. Using AI and Machine Learning (ML) algorithms, WaveSuite can:

- **Diagnose Problems:** Uncover the root sources of signal degradation.

- **Identify Threats:** Detect potential disruptions to the fiber infrastructure.

- **Automate Recovery:** Trigger fault notifications and recommend or initiate corrective actions to maintain performance.

This creates a "smarter" network that senses, thinks, and acts, ensuring high-speed services remain resilient and adaptable.

## **Part of a Continuous Evolution**

The PSE-6s is not a standalone innovation but the latest step in Nokia’s decade-long history of optical leadership, which began with the introduction of the first 100Gb/s coherent optics. It is a central component of Nokia's mission to simplify scale and operations through an end-to-end portfolio that includes **market-leading C+L band line systems**, **P-OTN switching**, and **network automation software**.

In conclusion, the Nokia PSE-6s represents the next leap in super-coherent optics. By breaking through the limitations of current technology, it offers a path forward for network operators to seize the opportunities of the 800GE era. With its focus on **unprecedented scale, game-changing performance, and a massive reduction in power consumption**, the PSE-6s is built to ensure that the networks of tomorrow are ready for the bandwidth demands of the future. For those looking to build a network that is both powerful and sustainable, the math has never looked more favorable.
